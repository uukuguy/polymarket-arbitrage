<threat_model>
  <assets>
    - Paper-account balance, realized PnL, and open-position projection.
    - Applied-operation identity namespace and stored result receipts.
  </assets>
  <trust_boundaries>
    - Caller-provided `--operation-id` enters through CLI/Makefile and is treated only as an opaque parameterized-SQL value.
    - Future venue `fill_id` enters through the Fill model; it identifies a receipt but does not authorize an order.
  </trust_boundaries>
  <threats>
    - ID collision/reuse could replay another market's result: validate operation type and target and exit non-zero on mismatch.
    - Corrupt result JSON/type could forge success: propagate JSON/storage errors and require a close receipt result to be float but not bool.
    - Concurrent retry could double-book: keep `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` plus unique ledger ID in `apply()` as final authority.
    - SQL injection through IDs: use only parameterized queries; never interpolate caller identity into SQL.
    - Scope expansion into secrets/live orders: no venue API, wallet, credential, signing, network, or reconciliation calls are added.
  </threats>
</threat_model>
