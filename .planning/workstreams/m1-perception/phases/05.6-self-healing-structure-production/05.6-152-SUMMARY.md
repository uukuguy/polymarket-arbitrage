# 05.6-152 Summary — Clean quarantined source-window queue residue

## Delivered

- Quarantining one source page now atomically marks all unleased sibling pages
  in the same window (`runnable`, `retryable`, `checkpointed`) quarantined too.
- In-flight leased siblings are deliberately left fenced to finish or fail on
  their own lease; they are never forcibly reassigned.

## Why

- A quarantined staging source window could retain thousands of unclaimable
  runnable/retryable jobs. Claim filtering protected new work, but queue health
  counts and oldest-work signals remained misleading.

## Verification

- Real-Postgres RED/GREEN contract proves an unleased exact-batch sibling is
  terminally quarantined in the same operation.
- Focused source/scheduler/CLI/PostgreSQL suite and Ruff passed.
