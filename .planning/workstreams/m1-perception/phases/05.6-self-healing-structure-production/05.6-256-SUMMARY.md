# Plan 05.6-256 Summary: Repair the Post-Publication Opportunity Path

Production timestamps falsified the earlier assumption that the 24-minute
Structure parity pass consumed qualification freshness. Structure was published
at 18:31:15, Quote at 18:42:35, and Opportunity at 18:46:19. The actual
post-publication critical path was 680 + 224 = 904 seconds, four seconds beyond
the unchanged 900-second product SLO.

The 224-second Opportunity step loaded 143 certified Quote batches serially.
`TransactionalOpportunityCertifier` now reads immutable batch references and
authenticated R2 artifacts in ordered waves. Production obtains the existing
`clob_batch_max_concurrency` authority (default 12, validated 1..32); constructor
default one preserves isolated adapters and tests unless they opt in.

The certifier still owns one lease and performs one terminal pointer publication.
The outer runtime synchronization continues lease heartbeat while a wave runs.
Each lane checks service stop before DB/R2 work, the executor drains started
siblings before returning, and `executor.map` restores frozen input order even
when reads complete out of order. Stop remains an interruption and does not
consume retry budget.

TDD proves 24 batches reach exactly six active reads, preserve leg and quote
order, reject zero or greater-than-32 concurrency, and leave no projection
threads behind. The complete Opportunity, CLI, and runtime-contract regression
set passes.

## Production boundary

The exact `67a20275b4378def8531cdf9e06d77c9b7edbf20` source was built only through
the command-scoped OrbStack socket and published as immutable digest
`sha256:3001803a58622578aa479c17e14745bb9de5afa6cd9c71bdb4c8ab25dcbd2d08`.
All eight formal Machines were replaced in place with that digest and retained
their exact IDs, process roles, `SIGTERM/40s` envelope and observe-only recovery
configuration. The public API remained available after the rollout; the exact
topology and secret-name-only audit passed with zero expired leases and zero open
circuits.

The first successor lineage published Structure generation
`04b7220f7835d30be5f1bf8dc06ce7a340129c941b396286b63f8ac2fca9b01a` at
`20:16:56.342386Z`, Quote at `20:27:32.833400Z`, and started Opportunity at
`20:27:33.363474Z`. The Opportunity attempt was terminal-success before the
`20:28:21Z` idle observation. Therefore Structure-to-Opportunity was less than
685 seconds, leaving more than 215 seconds beneath the unchanged 900-second SLO;
the formerly 224-second serial Opportunity segment completed in less than 48
seconds in production.

The rollout also exposed a separate downstream consumer-capacity defect:
qualification was configured for 100 facts every 30 seconds and slept even when
every read was full. It could therefore remain behind the new freshness fact
despite successful publication. That issue is owned by Plan 257; this plan does
not mislabel qualification lag as an Opportunity publication failure.

One operator read was incorrectly executed by starting a second Python process
inside the 256 MB qualification Machine. Fly OOM-restarted the resident process,
which resumed the same epoch and cursor with a 101-second maximum gap and no
breaker. All subsequent observation used the public control API or Fly logs.
Status commands must never be co-located with a resident process on a 256 MB
node.
