# Plan 05.6-34 Summary: Bound online Structure page commits

- Production stderr proved the latest Structure child reached
  `gamma-markets/commit` in milliseconds and then waited until its 75-second
  parent kill. The staging commit used SQLiteStore's offline 120-second busy

  retries the same durable cursor.

  bounded online Structure slices opt into this shorter deadline.

  advancement tests plus changed-file Ruff passed. Production checkpoint or
