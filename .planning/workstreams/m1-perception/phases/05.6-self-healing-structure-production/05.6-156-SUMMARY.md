# 05.6-156 Summary — Keep materializer failures inside the worker service

## Delivered

- A failed Structure materializer turn now writes its fenced retry incident and
  returns `retryable`; it no longer terminates the scheduler process.
- The incident detail carries a bounded source error message for operator
  diagnosis without exposing credentials.

## Why

- Staging exposed a `StructureSourceError` after an event-only window sealed.
  Re-raising it made Fly restart the whole service, stopping unrelated source,
  range, and Quote recovery work instead of preserving job-level failure truth.

## Verification

- RED/GREEN materializer contract proves unavailable immutable evidence records
  a retryable outcome and retained diagnostic text.
- Focused Gamma/source/materializer/scheduler/PostgreSQL/CLI suite (140 tests),
  Ruff, and diff check passed before staging deployment.
