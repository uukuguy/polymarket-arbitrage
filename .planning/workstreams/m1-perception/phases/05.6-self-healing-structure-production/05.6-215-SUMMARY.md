# 05.6-215 Summary — Close the open-circuit/expired-lease recovery deadlock

## Outcome

- Circuit-probe admission now defers a live lease on its own target before any
  action, idempotency, incident, or recovery-budget row is written.
- A due probe may absorb its own expired lease only under the exact persisted
  attempt and lease fence. The expired attempt transition and probe release
  commit atomically in one PostgreSQL transaction.
- Sibling active actions, leased jobs, and released probe windows continue to
  serialize the singleton worker lane. No timeout was added or enlarged.

## Root cause

- `RuntimeReconciler` correctly gives an open circuit authority over its job
  lifecycle and therefore selects `probe-circuit` before expired-lease reclaim.
- The probe executor previously accepted only `retryable` or `runnable` jobs.
  When the attempt that opened the circuit later lost its lease, the job stayed
  `leased`; every probe became `stale-noop`, while the circuit priority made a
  separate `reclaim-job` decision unreachable.
- A scheduled stale-noop had already consumed one episode-scoped recovery
  action. Repetition could exhaust the budget without creating a new business
  attempt: a deterministic state-machine deadlock, not a slow-operation
  timeout.

## Safety boundary

- Admission rejects the same target while its lease is missing an expiry or
  has not expired. This rejection happens before all durable side effects.
- Execution locks the circuit, job, runtime projection, and exact attempt and
  validates `attempt_id`, `lease_epoch`, `lease_owner`, and expiry.
- Only an expired `running` or `checkpointed` attempt can be absorbed. A
  running attempt becomes `retryable` with `RecoveryLeaseExpired`; the job
  lease is cleared, runtime recovery is recorded, and the probe window is
  released within the same transaction.
- Any fence change remains a stale failure. There is no manual database repair,
  resident Machine SSH, broader worker concurrency, or timeout inflation.

- GREEN: the regression proves a live exact-target lease creates zero action
  and budget rows, then proves the expired lease is reclaimed and exactly one
  budget unit releases the probe.
- The existing three-stage singleton-lane test still passes for pending action,

- The recovery/circuit/probe/reclaim PostgreSQL group passes in full; Ruff,
