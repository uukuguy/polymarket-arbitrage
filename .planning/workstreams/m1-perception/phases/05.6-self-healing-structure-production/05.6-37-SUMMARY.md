# 05.6-37 — Bound event-member sidecar cleanup

## Outcome

The Structure event-member supervisor now has a finite cleanup path after a
child exceeds its execution deadline.  After `SIGTERM`, then `SIGKILL`, it
waits only one additional bounded interval for stdio draining.  If that drain
remains wedged, it cancels the parent-local reader and returns the existing
durable timeout classification so the scheduler can release its producer slot
and retry.

## Production evidence that motivated the change

The Fly authority machine showed a live
`structure-event-members-advance` sidecar beyond its cooperative 45-second
slice.  Its timeout coincided with `runtime:health_read_lane` unavailable and
`/arbitrage/opportunities` 503 responses.  The incident and operator console
remained visible, and Polywatch delivered the P1, coverage, and opportunity
unavailable alerts.  This is a supervision cleanup issue, not an absence of
Dashboard evidence.

## Contract

- A killed sidecar cannot make the scheduler wait forever on `communicate()`.
- The child is still signaled before the local pipe reader is cancelled.
- The result remains `structure-event-members-timeout`, preserving the
  existing incident/retry chain rather than pretending recovery succeeded.
- The Dashboard must render a read-model failure as an operator-visibility
  fault, never as zero incidents.
