# Plan 05.6-25 Summary: Yield stale Quote work to Structure publication

- When a completed Quote child is rejected because the published Structure
  universe is stale, the worker now waits up to five minutes instead of
  repeatedly consuming the shared SQLite producer lane.
- Structure publication calls `quote_worker.request_now()` on pointer switch,
  so a fresh Structure generation wakes Quote immediately rather than waiting
  for the bounded fallback delay.
- Focused worker coverage proves the stale-universe backoff while preserving
  the durable incident path.
