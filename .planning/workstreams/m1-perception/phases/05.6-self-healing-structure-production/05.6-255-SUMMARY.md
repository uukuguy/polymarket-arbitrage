## Newly exposed capacity contradiction

The new v2 qualification epoch correctly refused to inherit the old release's
31 seconds, but paused immediately on freshness. The live 1,088-range Structure
parity pass took about 24 minutes while its consumed-publication freshness SLO
is 900 seconds. Earlier work made Structure ranges and Quote batches concurrent;
the certifier itself remained serial.

The repair keeps one fenced certifier and one terminal publication transaction.
Within that lease, immutable R2 parity reads execute in waves bounded by the
existing `structure_range_max_concurrency` setting. Waves stop at each 25-range
checkpoint boundary. Only a fully drained successful wave advances progress;
a failure drains started siblings and can replay only that uncommitted wave.

TDD on 1,117 ranges proves 12 concurrent reads, exact resume after checkpoint
300, bounded replay of only the failed wave, and no reread of the committed
prefix. Service-stop and lease-renewal tests remain green. The combined
Structure/runtime/qualification/performance/CLI suite, Ruff and Pyright pass.

## Production boundary

The bounded parity code is locally complete but not yet in production. The
qualification worker remains online in `accumulating + paused`; it records no
eligible time while current product freshness is outside SLO. A new exact image
must be built with OrbStack, rolled without interrupting an active certifier,
then proved on a fresh lineage before eligible seconds may be claimed.
