# Plan 05.6-258 Summary: Scope Freshness Recovery to the Blocked Product

The rolling policy correctly paused an epoch when Structure, Quote or Opportunity
exceeded its freshness SLO, but the pause had no truthful recovery transition. A
later healthy freshness observation remained blocked, while any unrelated
contained recovery could clear the shared pending flag. The same epoch could
therefore be stranded or accidentally unlocked by a different subsystem.

The policy now compares the active eligibility reason with the explicit product
on each freshness fact. `freshness.structure` is cleared only by a Structure fact
whose measured age is within its declared SLO. Fresh Quote and Opportunity facts,
generic `recovery.retry`, and generic `recovery.confirmed` cannot change that
pause. Non-freshness recovery flows retain their prior contained/confirmed
transition.

The confirmation fact clears the pause without adding the paused interval to
coverage. A later healthy interval begins effective-time accumulation. Runtime
folding and constructor replay implement the same rule, so restart reconstruction
cannot disagree with the live state machine.

This closes the recovery-transition defect only. It does not prove that the
production Structure cadence can continually publish within 900 seconds; that
source-window/product-identity design remains the next explicit audit rather than
being hidden by a timeout increase.
