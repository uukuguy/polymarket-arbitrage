# 05.6-157 Summary — Preserve active-market semantics for event-only evidence

## Delivered

- Event-only Structure materialization now ignores nested children unless their
  source state is exactly `active=true` and `closed=false`.
- Closed embedded children remain in the immutable R2 event receipt, but cannot
  enter the active Structure market component.

## Why

- Staging materialization exposed a real Gamma event containing closed market
  `516950` under an active event. The historical child omitted `negRisk`, while
  the legacy v1 active `/markets` stream would not have returned it. Passing it
  to the strict truth validator correctly failed the bundle.

## Verification

- Regression contract uses one active event with both closed and open embedded
  children; only the open child appears in the v2 bundle.
- Source bundle/materializer suite (7 tests) and Ruff passed before deployment.
