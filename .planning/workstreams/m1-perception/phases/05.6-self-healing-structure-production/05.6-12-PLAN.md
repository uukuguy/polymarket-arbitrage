# Plan 05.6-12: Certified opportunity-read fallback availability

**Goal:** Keep the M1→M2 opportunity API available when its expensive live
source-health diagnostic times out, without accepting unauthenticated or stale
market truth.

## Requirements

- [x] A fresh certified Quote projection makes a source-read fallback an
  explicit operational warning rather than a synthetic strict-health failure.
- [x] Source binding invalidity, stale Quote/universe data, and unavailable
  authority still fail closed through their existing gates.
- [ ] Deploy and verify a live fallback returns HTTP 200 plus `warn`, not 503.

## Safety

No fallback constructs a new market identity. It uses only the source snapshot,
universe hash and freshness already sealed into the certified Quote projection.
