  expire while a timed-out predecessor still owned cancellation and SQLite
  cleanup. A replacement child could then overlap that tail, turning one
  transient upstream delay into a second writer-contended failure.

  the outer watchdog: collection, certification, and compact-feed publication
  are one ownership interval. The child collector remains independently capped
  at its collection hard limit.

  the 300-second Quote freshness SLA for both the worker and supervisor.

  Ruff passed. This repair still requires a deployed natural-cycle proof before
