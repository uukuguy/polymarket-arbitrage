# 05.6-234 Summary — Prove incomplete Quote batch recovery

## Outcome

- Added a disposable `quote-batch-incomplete` commissioning adapter for the
  `quote-certify` node. It admits two immutable Quote batches, commits the first
  terminal receipt and sends the second through the real fenced retry/incident
  transaction without publishing its receipt.
- The incomplete state proves two inputs, one receipt, one retryable producer,
  a waiting certifier, one open warning incident and one pending Dashboard alert.
  It also proves zero Quote manifest, zero current pointer and zero downstream
  opportunity job.
- At the shared retry policy's persisted due time, a replacement epoch commits
  the exact missing batch receipt, closes the producer incident and releases
  exactly one certifier turn. Certification commits one manifest, one current
  Quote pointer and one runnable opportunity successor.

## Design boundary

- The certifier barrier does not invent another timeout. Readiness is the
  conjunction of every immutable batch receipt and every producer job's
  `succeeded` state. A failed producer reports through the existing retry,
  circuit, incident and alert transaction.
- Injection and recovery use public fenced control-plane APIs only. SQL is
  read-only and verifies cardinality, state, incident lifecycle and absence of
  partial publication; no job, receipt, lease, clock, incident or pointer row is
  mutated privately.
- The failure blocks the affected generation but does not invalidate prior
  certified truth. The old Quote pointer remains authoritative until a complete
  new generation can atomically replace it.
