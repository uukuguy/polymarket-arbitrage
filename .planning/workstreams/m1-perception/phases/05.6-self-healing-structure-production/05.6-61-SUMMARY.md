# 05.6-61 Summary — Quote orphan lease reclaim

Rolling restart could leave a Quote producer lease valid for its 210-second
supervisor window. The replacement Quote child waited for that lease and left
the production opportunity feed stale despite having been successfully
spawned.

`ProducerArbitrator.relinquish_orphaned_quote_lease` atomically removes only a
Quote-owned lease and records a durable `released` receipt. `worker_cli` calls
it before constructing the supervised Quote worker and logs the reclaim. The
tests prove immediate same-owner recovery, Structure protection, and entry
point ordering.
