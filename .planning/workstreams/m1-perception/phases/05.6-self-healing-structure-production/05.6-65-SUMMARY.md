# 05.6-65 Summary — event-member query deadline

The event-member slice previously checked its 45-second budget only between
chunks. A long group-truth SQL query could therefore exceed the child budget
and be killed by the 75-second outer watchdog.

Each event-member chunk now receives its remaining slice budget and installs it
as a SQLite progress-handler deadline, including the separate group-truth
connection. A deadline interruption returns a durable `deadline` defer receipt;
the scheduler records that exact cause and retries through its existing bounded
defer loop instead of generating an unclassified child timeout.
