# Plan 05.6-21 Summary: Bounded health evidence reads

- Production diagnosis found `/healthz` taking 6.8–21 seconds while Structure
  and Quote competed for the 44 GB SQLite volume; Fly's service check timed
  out waiting for headers, which also made the dashboard effectively unusable.
- Health aggregation now uses its own single-slot `BoundedReadLane` with an
  0.8-second response budget. A timeout or occupied lane becomes the explicit
  `runtime:health_read_lane` P1 check: `read-model-unavailable` or
  `read-model-saturated`, with credential-free impact, automatic action, and
  operator action.
- `/health` returns HTTP 503 for that P1 to drive alerting. `/healthz` retains
  HTTP 200 solely to keep Fly routing recoverable, while its body reports
  `status=fail`; no read failure is represented as an all-clear.
- RED/GREEN tests cover saturated strict health and timed-out Fly probe
  semantics. The full health endpoint suite and changed-file Ruff pass.
