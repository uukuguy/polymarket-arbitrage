- Staging showed the terminal worker could still freeze before its batch-planning
  timeout while synchronously uploading its final R2 source artifact.
- Moved source artifact PUT+HEAD into a thread and bounded it to 90 seconds,
  matching the terminal historical-artifact read bound.
