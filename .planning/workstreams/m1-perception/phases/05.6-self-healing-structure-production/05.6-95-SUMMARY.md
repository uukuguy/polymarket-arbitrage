# 05.6-95 Summary — Enforce Structure component-count parity

## Delivered

- Certification now obtains frozen component counts from the admitted legacy
  Structure identity and compares them to the sum of all durable range
  receipts for each admitted component.
- A generation that is receipt-complete but drops rows (or claims extra rows)
  cannot produce a manifest.  The check occurs before accepting the manifest
  digest or terminalizing the certifier lease.
- The canonical manifest serializer is shared by R2 manifest creation and the
  repository digest calculation, preventing two competing manifest encodings.

## Verification

- New real Postgres adversarial case records a complete-looking zero-row
  event result against a frozen count of one; certification rejects it.
- Full focused Structure/control-plane regression: 38 passed; Ruff passes.

## Boundary

Count parity is not content parity.  The next gate must authenticate and
re-read range artifacts, prove their canonical headers/rows reassemble the
sealed source bundle, and only then consider a shadow publication pointer.
