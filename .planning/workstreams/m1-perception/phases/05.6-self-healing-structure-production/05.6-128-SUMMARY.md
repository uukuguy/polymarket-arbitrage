# 05.6-128 Summary — Separate alert intent policy from Telegram credentials

## Delivered

- Added non-secret `POLYARB_ALERT_CHANNELS` policy to select transactional
  alert outbox channels.
- Configured the data-worker Fly template to emit dashboard and Telegram
  intents without receiving Telegram credentials.
- Kept Telegram token/chat ID exclusively meaningful to the isolated alert
  sender, preserving least privilege and durable delivery replay.

## Verification

  token still produced dashboard-only channels under the old implementation.
- GREEN: transactional alert, worker and deployment-template tests pass;
  Ruff, `make docs-m1-check`, and `make planning-status` pass.

created or changed.
