# 05.6-66 Summary — immediate snapshot after member sealing

After event-member/group-truth evidence sealed, the scheduler treated that
sidecar result as a completed normal tick and waited the five-minute cadence
before attempting the fresh Structure snapshot required for recovery proof.

Every non-deferred member checkpoint, including a sealed one, now sets the
existing checkpoint continuation flag. The next two-second resident tick still
passes Quote arbitration, then immediately admits the fresh snapshot. The
regression proves a writer-busy retry followed by sealing retains this
continuation instead of going idle.
