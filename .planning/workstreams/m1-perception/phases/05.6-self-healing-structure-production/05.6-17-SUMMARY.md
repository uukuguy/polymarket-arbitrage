# Plan 05.6-17 Summary: Structure page boundary diagnosis

  the `gamma-markets` stage marker alone could not identify whether Gamma fetch
  or the durable SQLite commit owned the remaining stall.

  breadcrumbs for `fetch` and `commit` start/complete. Parent timeout evidence
  retains the final boundary only; arbitrary child output remains redacted.

  previously introduced `structure-page-deadline`, closing a persistence gap
  that could have rejected an otherwise safe child diagnosis.

  Structure-sync and attempt-status suites plus Ruff pass. A new Fly release
