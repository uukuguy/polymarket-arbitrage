## Operator Surface

- **Runtime overview:** canonical controller healthy/critical/unavailable,
  epoch/tick/lease, nested missing-controller as not healthy or empty, and
  Quote pointer / Structure manifest freshness from server `published_at`.
- **Active tasks:** job/stage/recovery state/progress, server-provided
  heartbeat/progress ages, and controller/heartbeat/progress/recovery
  deadlines with overdue status.
- **Incident timeline:** stable incident and action keys, transition
  `reason_code`/impact, associated recovery action type/state/result, and an
  independent Recent recovery actions table for resolved/unlinked completions.
- **Rolling qualification:** server eligible/required progress, four-state
  status, last breaker, policy identity, last fact, and certificate anchors with
  digest. Certificate links are page anchors only; no external endpoint is
  invented.

## Alerting and Recovery Semantics

- Task-local facts remain the primary source for known task results; watchdog
  checks are the fallback for missing heartbeat/progress, expired leases,
  overdue deadlines, or controller unavailability.
- durable incident records the operator/audit lifecycle; outbox records
  delivery attempts. Telegram delivery success does not prove user read.
- Recovery mutation remains fenced by controller/job/action/attempt/lease
  identity. Dashboard and smoke targets are read-only.
- break-glass is an explicit operator posture, but when the writer is
  unavailable it can only use the generic one-shot direct page and the
  ledger/Dashboard may be stale. After writer recovery, incident/action/outbox
  facts must be reconciled before claiming operator closure.
