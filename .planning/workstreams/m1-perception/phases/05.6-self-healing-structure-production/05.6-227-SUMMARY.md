# 05.6-227 Summary — Prove live-lease progress-stall recovery

## Outcome

- Added a cleanup-safe `progress-stall` commissioning adapter for all eight M1
  production nodes. It holds the real node transaction after its durable
  progress checkpoint while continuing the same attempt's formal heartbeat.
- Detection reads the persisted runtime profile and routes the exact candidate
  through `RuntimeReconciler`; it accepts only the non-breaking
  `job.progress-stalled -> cancel-job` decision while the renewed lease remains
  live.
- The real `RecoveryExecutor` executes the database-fenced action. The old
  attempt becomes `RecoveryProgressStalled/retryable`, loses its lease and
  cannot publish a terminal fact.
- After the policy-owned durable backoff, a successor lease epoch completes the
  same prepared business transaction. Its success resolves the exact warning
  incident through `record_job_recovery` and produces a causal business
  postcondition receipt.

## Executable workflow

- Generalized the isolated commissioning harness without changing its one
  migrated database per node or cleanup-ownership rules.
- Added `make m1-production-commissioning-progress-stall`, requiring an explicit
  loopback-only test DSN plus exact release/config identity. The CLI still has
  no production DSN fallback, node-skip option or all-run timeout.
- Registered H-041 and added the eight-node adapter, isolated one-node harness
  and exact Make argv tests to the deterministic production-enablement climb
  profile.

## Chain-truth evidence

- Eight real-PostgreSQL node tests prove exactly two attempts: epoch 1 is
  retryable with `RecoveryProgressStalled`; epoch 2 is succeeded.
- Each test proves one `job.recovery-started` fact whose reason is
  `job.progress-stalled`, one completed successful `cancel-job` action, one
  warning incident that becomes resolved, one recovered incident event and one
  node-specific business postcondition.
- The adapter derives progress, heartbeat, lease and action-worker bounds from
  the persisted runtime deadline profile. Logical stage receipt ordering is not
  an orchestration timeout.

## Boundary

- No production database, provider, Fly deployment, Machine, SSH, Docker
  context or Colima operation is part of this plan.
- H-041 confirmation and exact-image eight-node evidence follow this commit.
  Commissioning remains incomplete after progress-stall; heartbeat-outage,
  worker-exit, retry-budget and node-specific obligations still gate production
  canaries and qualification.
