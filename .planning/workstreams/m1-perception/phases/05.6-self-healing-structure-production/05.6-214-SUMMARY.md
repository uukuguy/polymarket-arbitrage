# 05.6-214 Summary — Keep Structure parity CPU work inside the lease boundary

## Outcome

- The synchronous Structure certifier now runs every payload-sized parse,
  comparison, digest construction, reassembly, and manifest-artifact build
  through its existing lease-renewing bounded call.
- No timeout was enlarged or added. The 30-second lease still derives the
  10-second heartbeat cadence, and the existing 3,600-second attempt deadline
  remains the absolute completion authority.
- A deterministic regression blocks v3 shard parsing beyond one lease and
  proves at least two durable heartbeats occur before the parser is released.

## Production evidence and root cause

- Exact v20 released the named Structure certifier circuit with action
  `88f4fb22-66a3-49a0-9e0e-d80ad6362c47`; the action completed successfully,
  consumed one episode-scoped budget, and reported zero publication pointer
  mutations.
- Attempt `502de05d-7c55-4c7f-a430-5147d709e093` advanced linearly from
  `verify-parity=235/1117` to `756/1117` with all four overdue fields at zero.
  It then stopped heartbeat and progress while the coordinator Machine and all
  sibling lanes remained started and active.
- R2 reads were already wrapped by `_run_bounded_sync_call`, but
  `parse_structure_shard_bytes`, `parse_structure_range_bytes`, large row
  equality, legacy reassembly, prefix-digest construction, and manifest
  artifact construction ran directly in the outer certifier thread. A large
  shard could therefore spend more than one lease in CPU work without a
  heartbeat. The next fenced renewal became stale, the scheduler correctly
  stopped the stale worker, and the still-open circuit prevented an automatic
  expired-lease reclaim.
- Fly Machine events proved there was no restart, replacement, OOM, or process
  stop. This is an internal lease-boundary defect rather than platform
  instability.
