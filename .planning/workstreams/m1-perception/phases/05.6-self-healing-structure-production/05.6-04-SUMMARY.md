# Plan 05.6-04 Summary: Certification-stage health truth

## Root cause

Production scheduler logs reported `stage=certifying`, while `/health` exposed
the old normalization fields as `stage=writing/issues|done`. The durable
`structure_publications` record already had the certification component and
cursor; `structure_generation_status()` simply omitted them from its read-only
projection. This made the health/alert boundary unable to distinguish active
certification from a stuck normalizer.

## Delivered

- Added `certification_component` and `certification_cursor` to the bounded,
  read-only generation status projection.
- Health now reports `stage=certifying` whenever an active writing publication
  has entered durable certification, while retaining the same checkpoint-SLA
  failure semantics.
- The health output includes both certification component and cursor for direct
  operator and Polywatch evidence.

## Verification

- Green: targeted contract and complete health endpoint test file passed.
- Ruff passed for all changed files.
