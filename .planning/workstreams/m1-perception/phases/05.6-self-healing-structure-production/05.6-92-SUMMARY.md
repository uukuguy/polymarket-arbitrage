## Boundary

This initial shadow admission creates one full range for each component.  It
is sufficient to exercise the transaction/recovery path, but production
admission must split large components into immutable stable-key ranges before
continuous scheduling.  Certification, manifest parity, and pointer gating
remain unimplemented.
