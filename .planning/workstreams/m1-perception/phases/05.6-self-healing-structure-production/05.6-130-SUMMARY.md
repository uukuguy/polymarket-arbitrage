# 05.6-130 Summary — Fail-close transactional source page ceiling

## Delivered

- Added a `max_pages` ceiling (default 1000) to the transactional Structure
  source worker, aligned with the Gamma client's documented page bound.
- On an over-limit page, the worker performs no Gamma/R2 request. It atomically
  quarantines its fenced job and active source window in Postgres, preventing
  partial materialization or publication and allowing a later cadence bucket to
  admit a fresh window.
