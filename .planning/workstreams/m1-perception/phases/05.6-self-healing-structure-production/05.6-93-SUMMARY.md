  frozen Structure generation.
- `certify_structure_generation` verifies every admitted range has exactly
  one receipt with the admitted bundle/component/range identity, computes the
  ordered manifest digest from immutable range artifacts, and only then writes
  `m1_generation_manifests` under the certifier's lease fence.

  and stale certifier epochs fail before a manifest can be durable.  This
  operation deliberately makes no publication-pointer change.
