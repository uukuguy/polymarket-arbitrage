# 05.6-149 Summary — Prioritize due retryable control-plane jobs

## Delivered

- Durable job claiming now prioritizes a retryable job whose backoff has
  elapsed over newly admitted runnable work.
- The ordering remains deterministic within each priority class and does not
  alter lease expiry recovery, retry backoff, fencing, or the eight-lane source
  concurrency bound.

## Why

- A real staging source window accumulated 53 due `PaginationIntegrityError`
  jobs behind thousands of older, already-runnable market batches. The prior
  timestamp-only ordering could postpone their reclaim until the entire initial
  batch drained, delaying terminal materialization despite a healthy service.

## Verification

- RED: a real-Postgres contract showed a due retryable job lost to newer
  runnable work under the former ordering.
- GREEN: the same contract passes after the priority change.
- Full control-plane source, scheduler, CLI, and PostgreSQL suite passed.
- Ruff passed for the changed production and test files.
