# 05.6-194 Summary — opportunity projection current-pointer short circuit

## Outcome

The opportunity certifier now reads the transactional `opportunity:current` pointer
before loading any Quote artifact. When it already names the current Quote generation,
the certifier returns `current` and performs no R2 reads. A newly certified Quote still
follows the complete authenticated projection path.

- Focused opportunity and real-Postgres projection tests: 5 passed.
- Ruff format and lint checks passed.

## Operational effect

The formal two-second coordinator loop no longer repeatedly scans all frozen Quote
batches after it has atomically published the current projection.
