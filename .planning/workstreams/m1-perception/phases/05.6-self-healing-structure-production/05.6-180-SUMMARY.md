# 05.6-180 — Structure certifier 长运行租约续租

## Root cause

Live staging revealed that a complete large Structure generation can spend
more than its 30-second certification lease rebuilding content parity from
hundreds of R2 range artifacts. The old certifier held one epoch while doing
that work, so its lease expired even though the process was still computing.

## Change

- `TransactionalStructureCertifier` now has a monotonic-clock keepalive gate
  at one third of its existing lease duration.
- Each R2/parity segment invokes the gate; a due gate calls the existing
  fenced `PostgresControlPlane.heartbeat` with the same owner and epoch.
- The renewed lease is used for final manifest certification and recovery.
  A rejected heartbeat remains a `StaleLeaseError`, so process-loss fencing is
  preserved and the 30-second takeover bound is not inflated.

## Verification

- The new certifier test first failed because the constructor did not expose
  a monotonic clock/heartbeat path, then passed after implementation.
- `uv run pytest tests/m1-perception/test_transactional_structure_worker.py -q` — 8 passed.
- Changed-file Ruff passed.

## Live follow-up

Deploy only the coordinator staging Machine. Its restart fences the old
expired epoch and lets the new certifier reclaim the still-unpublished
generation; verify that the reclaimed epoch stays unexpired throughout
parity and eventually reaches a terminal state before resuming the v2 soak.
