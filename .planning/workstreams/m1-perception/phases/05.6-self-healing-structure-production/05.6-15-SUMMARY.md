# Plan 05.6-15 Summary: Bounded Gamma page deadline

- Live v318 evidence showed that correcting the 45/75-second admission
  mismatch was necessary but insufficient: Gamma market-page work could still
  remain inside its own retry loop until the parent killed the Structure child
  at 75 seconds. The durable attempt recorded only a stage-start marker.
- A bounded Structure slice now gives one remote Gamma page at most 35 seconds,
  preserving the established 45-second cooperative slice semantics while
  reserving time for its SQLite checkpoint and orderly process exit.
- A deadline becomes the allowlisted, redacted
  `structure-page-deadline` child failure, so the scheduler, incident API, and
  operator console retain a specific diagnosis rather than a parent-kill
  symptom. The existing recovery loop remains active; this change does not
  hide or close the P1.
- RED/GREEN tests prove a hung market page is cancelled before parent kill and
  that the CLI emits the new bounded failure kind. Structure-sync, CLI JSON,
  and scheduler regression suites plus Ruff passed before deployment.
