# 05.6-56 Summary — actionable Quote supervisor diagnostics

Supervisor failures now persist a safe, stable reason token in their bounded
receipt: `supervisor-spawn-error:<ExceptionType>` for launch failures and
`supervisor-control-error:<ExceptionType>` for failures after a child exists.
The potentially secret-bearing exception message is never persisted.

`/perception/producer-progress` and the Fly-native console expose the latest
Quote supervisor receipt through a single-row query. This joins the child
attempt, feed hydration, and supervisor boundary in the operator view instead
of requiring SSH to diagnose a P1.

During a Fly stop, the supervisor now retries a transient SQLite-locked
terminal receipt in a bounded five-attempt window.  If the volume cannot be
written before shutdown, cancellation remains authoritative instead of being
replaced by `OperationalError`; startup reconciliation can then classify the
unclosed reservation from durable evidence.
