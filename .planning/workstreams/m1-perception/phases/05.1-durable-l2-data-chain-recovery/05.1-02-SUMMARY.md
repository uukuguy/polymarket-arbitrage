## Decisions Made

- The database projection is authoritative for history, so every refresh reads
  active durable keys instead of trusting process-local diffs.
- The process-local candidate set commits last. Partial WS or mirror work is safe
  because the next durable retry converges both sides idempotently.
- LISTEN disconnection warns, while stale business reconciliation or cursor lag
  beyond the configured 180-second grace fails strict health.
