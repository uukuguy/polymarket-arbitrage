- `async run_event_writer(stop_event: asyncio.Event, *, runtime: L3EvidenceRuntime, store: L3EvidenceStore, flush_interval_s: float = 1.0) -> None` persists queued real-time events in sequence order.
- Four strict check names: `l3:evidence_sample_age_seconds`, `l3:promoter_ledger_age_seconds`, `l3:membership_convergence`, and `l3:worst_market_freshness`.
