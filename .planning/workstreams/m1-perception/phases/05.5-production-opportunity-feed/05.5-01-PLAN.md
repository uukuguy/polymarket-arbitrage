# Plan 05.5-01: Production neg-risk opportunity feed

**Goal:** Run the H-009 quote collector continuously on the L1 app machine and
prove the public feed remains fresh across repeated production cycles.

**Approved design:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-26-production-neg-risk-quote-worker-design.md`

**Executable plan:** `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-26-production-neg-risk-quote-worker.md`

## Requirements

- [ ] Immediate attempt, 120-second cadence, no overlap, fail-soft retry, and
  cancellation propagation under TDD.
- [ ] Explicit production enablement in `fly.toml`; disabled elsewhere.
- [ ] Health reads durable complete-run time and fails above 300 seconds.
- [ ] L1 main owns quote-worker startup and shutdown.
- [ ] Focused/full local verification pass before deployment.
- [ ] Exact deployed SHA and image identity are recorded.
- [ ] Three successive complete production runs and repeated HTTP 200 responses.
- [ ] Manual, learning doc, JOURNAL, STATE, and SUMMARY are current.

## Makefile surface

- Reuse `make diagnose-arb-feed-prod min_edge_bps=0`.

## Safety

- Public CLOB reads and local quote-run writes only.
- No wallet, order, secret, migration, or VM-scaling operation.
- No fee-adjusted or real-money readiness claim.
