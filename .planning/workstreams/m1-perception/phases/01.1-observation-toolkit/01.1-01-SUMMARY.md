### JOIN demo（plan 03 scan-by-tag 实现样板）
```sql
SELECT m.slug, e.title, t.tag_label
FROM markets m
JOIN events e ON m.event_id = e.id AND m.snapshot_id = e.snapshot_id
JOIN event_tags t ON e.id = t.event_id AND e.snapshot_id = t.snapshot_id
WHERE m.liquidity_usd > 100000
ORDER BY m.liquidity_usd DESC
LIMIT 5;
```

输出（节选）：
```
will-iraq-win-the-2026-fifa-world-cup | 2026 FIFA World Cup Winner | Soccer
will-iraq-win-the-2026-fifa-world-cup | 2026 FIFA World Cup Winner | Sports
will-iraq-win-the-2026-fifa-world-cup | 2026 FIFA World Cup Winner | FIFA World Cup
```

3-way JOIN 工作正常（一个 market 通过 event_id → 一个 event → 多个 tags）。
