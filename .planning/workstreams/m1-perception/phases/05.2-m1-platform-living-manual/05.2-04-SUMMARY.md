# Phase 05.2 Plan 04: Canonical Manual Entry Points

Wired the M1 living manual into the repository's three canonical entry points
while making each document's authority explicit.

## Entry Points and Authority

- `README.md` now sends product and operations readers to the stable manual and
  sends changing deployment facts to `.planning/CURRENT.md`. The stale
  `M1 生产数据目前 unhealthy` statement was removed without replacing it
  with a frozen optimistic health claim.
- `docs/learning/00-INDEX.md` links operators to the manual and remains scoped
  to the learning sequence and code mental model.
- `.planning/CURRENT.md` uses the required `../docs/` relative path to the
  manual and remains scoped to volatile current state.

The focused contract test checks manual filename presence in `README.md` and
the exact relative paths used from `docs/learning/` and `.planning/CURRENT.md`.
